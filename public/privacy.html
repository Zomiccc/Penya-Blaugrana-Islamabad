Who We Are

Penya Blaugrana Islamabad ('PBI', 'we', or 'us') is dedicated to fostering friendship and collaboration among FC Barcelona socis and supporters. This policy explains how and why we use your personal information.

We take the security of your data very seriously and have robust measures in place to protect it. If you have any concerns about how we handle your information, please reach out using the contact details at the end of this statement.

This policy should be read alongside our Data Protection Procedures, available on our website.

The Information We Collect About You

We collect personal data from you when you:

  • Sign up for membership with us.
  • Contact us via email, phone, post, or social media.

The types of information we may collect include:

Type of DataDescription
Personal & Contact DetailsName, date of birth, address, email, phone number, country of origin, and signature.
Government IdentifiersPassport number.
Membership InformationMembership length, event attendance records ('activity points'), marketing preferences, and (if applicable) your FC Barcelona soci number (required for registration with FCB).
Visual ImagesPhotographs or videos taken at PBI events.
Lifestyle & PreferencesDemographic information and your favourite football club(s).
Survey, Competition & Promotion ResponsesRecords of your participation in surveys or competitions we run.
Marketing Preferences & ResponsesHow you interact with our communications and your requests to stop receiving them.

Most of the data we collect is necessary to meet our legal or contractual obligations. Where we ask for your consent to process data, you are not obliged to provide it. However, doing so may limit the services and benefits you can receive from us.

What We Use Your Data ForOur Legal Basis for Using It

Who We May Share Your Data With

We may need to share your information with other organisations and members to fulfil our obligations to you. We may also share data when it is in our legitimate interest to do so. These parties typically include:

  • FC Barcelona (for official registration of socis).
  • PBI partners who help us host events.
  • Membership card manufacturers.
  • Other PBI members (for games or activities we organise). Only specific details like your name, membership number, and activity points are shared.
  • FC Barcelona or PBI affiliates for marketing purposes. We will only share your data with other third-party organisations if you have given us your explicit consent to do so.

Where We May Transfer Your Data

PBI is based in Pakistan. In most cases, any third parties we share your data with are located within the country.

However, some event providers may be based outside Pakistan, in countries with different data protection standards. If we need to transfer your data to such a country (a 'Third Country'), we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data. We will also ask for your explicit consent before proceeding with the transfer.

Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, taking into account the potential risks to you. All information you provide to us is stored on secure servers.

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website. For more details, please see our separate Cookie Policy.

Marketing Communications and Your Choices

When you first sign up, we will ask if you would like to receive communications from us (usually via a tick box on the membership form). You cannot opt out at any time; certain communications are required to keep you informed as a member.

What does 'Opting Out' mean?

Opting out means you will no longer receive unsolicited marketing messages from us, including via email, phone, or mail. Please note: This will also stop you from receiving important information about events, matches, and promotions from PBI.

How to Opt Out

If you no longer wish to receive any communications from us, please email us at info@pbisb.com and request to be removed from our mailing list.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Request that we delete your data (in certain circumstances).
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time where we rely on it.

How to Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at:

Email: info@pbisb.com

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and, where appropriate, notified to you by email. We encourage you to check back periodically for the latest version.
