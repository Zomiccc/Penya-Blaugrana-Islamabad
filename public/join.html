The perks

What membership gets you

Joining Penya Blaugrana Islamabad means becoming part of Pakistan's vibrant FC Barcelona fan community.

We bring together passionate Barça supporters from across the country, united by a love for football and the values of our club.

Your membership helps us grow the community, organize events, and celebrate FC Barcelona in Pakistan.

  • Priority access to our venue for important matches
  • 20% discount at our venue on match days
  • Access to our members-only WhatsApp group
  • Connect with fellow FC Barcelona supporters across Pakistan
  • Meet new people and make many friends
