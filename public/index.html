Our passion

At Penya Blaugrana Islamabad, our passion is FC Barcelona. We are building a community in Pakistan where culers come together to celebrate the beautiful game, share unforgettable moments, and live the values of the club.

Through match screenings, cultural events and community gatherings, we bring Barça fans in Islamabad closer to the team we all love.

Our passion goes beyond the pitch. We proudly support FCB Femení and work to raise awareness of their achievements, and of women's football in Pakistan.

Our trips

Our goal is to create chances for fans to watch matches side by side, share the experience, and strengthen the bond between culers here.

Being part of the Barça family means joining penyas that bring supporters together across borders.

It's a real family. Friendships get built and memories get made through our love for the club.

Where we gather

As the newest addition to the worldwide Barça family, Penya Blaugrana Islamabad hosts match screenings for supporters across Pakistan. While we finalise our official home venue, the commitment stays the same: the best atmosphere to watch FC Barcelona outside Camp Nou.

From screenings to social meetups, every gathering is built on passion, energy and camaraderie. Whether you've supported Barça your whole life or you're just discovering the club, there's a place for you here.
