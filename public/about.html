Who We Are

Who We Are

Penya Blaugrana Islamabad is one of the newest FC Barcelona penyas, proudly bringing the Barça spirit to Pakistan and uniting supporters through football.

Our mission is to create a vibrant space for Barça fans in Islamabad and beyond — a place to socialise, build friendships, and share unforgettable match-day experiences together.

Penya Blaugrana Islamabad is the proud new home of Barça passion in Pakistan. Our penya brings together fans to celebrate the spirit of FC Barcelona while promoting its core values of respect, tolerance, sportsmanship, solidarity, and fair play.

Penya Blaugrana Islamabad (PBI) is a community of passionate FC Barcelona supporters in Pakistan. Our mission is to unite Barça fans by hosting match screenings, social events, and cultural activities that bring people closer to the club we all love.

At PBI, we celebrate football, friendship, and the values of FC Barcelona while creating unforgettable experiences for our members. Together, we aim to be the home of Barça supporters in Islamabad.
